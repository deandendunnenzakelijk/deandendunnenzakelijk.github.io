Why IT?

I chose ICT because I have always been interested in technology and the many possibilities it offers. There are so many different paths within ICT, which gives me the freedom to find something that fits me and my goals.

For me, my future is not only about having a job. I want to build a career that gives me a good work-life balance and the freedom to decide how I want to work. I believe ICT can help me achieve that.

During my HBO-ICT study, I want to develop more than just my programming skills. I also want to learn how to communicate with customers, work together with other developers, and gain real-world experience. I will keep improving myself, building my network, and learning new technologies.

My goal is to become a reliable software developer who can work for a company or independently, using modern technology to build, improve, and optimize systems that solve real problems.
